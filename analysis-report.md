
## Introduction

Subscription SaaS (Software-as-a-Service) businesses rely heavily on their pricing page to keep current users and attract new customers. It is an important touchpoint in the customer journey. A poorly set up pricing page can lead to disinterest and cause users to abandon the website, even if they are interested in the product.

In this hypothetical scenario, the company is looking to revamp its payment plan. Currently, the pricing page uses a monthly/annual toggle format that presents two plan options with minimal context.

It offers little support for prospective customers in the value of upgrading to higher-tier payment plans. The conversion rates for free trial users, as a result, have plateaued. If there were a more robust payment page, the pricing experience for users could improve.
